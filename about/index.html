A floor to start the next thing from.

Floa.One means floor one. The ground floor. A new beginning.

I spent twenty years riding the industry up, floor by floor. An ad agency, then games, then consumer software, then enterprise platforms built for the Fortune 1000. Each level taught me something the last one could not.

Image
The story

The work underneath

What held across all of them is that the screen is never the product. The interface is visible. The structures that enable it are not. The work that decides whether a product holds together happens underneath, in the systems, the platforms, the operating models, the connections that let teams and products move as one thing instead of many. That is the layer I kept returning to, and over time it became the thing I do best: finding the real problem while everyone is still solving the visible one, and connecting the parts so people can focus on the work instead of the fragmentation around them.

Where it started

It started with making things by hand, then making them for other people. Art first, where I learned that an idea is only worth as much as the form you can give it. Then client service, directing creative work for others, where I found out that making something land for someone else is a different craft than making it please yourself, and learned to listen for the problem underneath the brief, the one the client could feel but had not named. Somewhere in there, the making met technology, and the work changed shape. It stopped being about objects and started being about systems, the structures underneath the thing people actually touch. I followed that all the way up: from client work to product, from product to the system behind the products, from there to the platforms the Fortune 1000 runs its business on. The canvas kept getting bigger and less visible. The work never changed. Take something formless and give it a structure that holds.

Why this site

This site is a collection of that work, those lessons, and the principles that keep guiding how I practice. It carries everything I learned on the way up back down to the ground with me. That is where good foundations start.

Global experience
