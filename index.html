01

Strategy has to survive the work.

At Apple, a packaged product became a cloud platform over eight years. What carried the strategy through was a design practice and a coherent system strong enough to hold it together.

02

Systems should make teams stronger.

At ThoughtSpot, I built the platform design function alongside engineering: 140+ components, 8+ feature teams, 12K weekly Figma activities. The measure was never the component count. It was whether other teams became faster, clearer, and more independent.

03

Consistency is not sameness.

At Yahoo!, consistency meant rules that let different things belong together. I helped set a shared navigation model across a decade of disconnected surfaces, then led the design of Search, where that model had to work at scale. The hardest surface to fit proves the system is real.
