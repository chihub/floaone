What I do

Image

Product Strategy

Start with the customer. I balance their needs against business priorities to set a clear north star, then build and iterate quickly with real users. On one enterprise platform, a working prototype in three weeks ended a ten-year modernization stall.

Image

Design Leadership

Clear the path, then hold the bar. I lead distributed design teams alongside Product and Engineering, where each designer owns a problem space and works to one shared standard of quality. Peers describe our practice as the one that pulls teams together.

Image

Experience Systems

Solve problems with a system. I build the design systems and operating practices that connect fragmented experiences. Recent work includes a shared pattern library that teams now build on, and an AI-automated front end for a legacy enterprise platform.

01

Strategy has to survive the work.

A strategy proves itself years later, when the work ships. What carries it there is a design practice and a coherent system strong enough to hold the intent together.

02

Systems make teams stronger.

Measure a design system by what it does for the teams around it: whether they become faster, clearer, and more independent because it exists.

03

Consistency is not sameness.

Different things can belong together when they share a set of rules. The hardest surface to fit is the one that proves the system is real.
