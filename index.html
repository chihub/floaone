01

Strategy into execution

At Apple/Claris, a packaged product more than 30 years old became a cloud platform across 16 countries and 12+ languages. Eight years, six products from zero to one, one coherent system, held together by a design function built to sustain it.

02

Platforms that grow

At ThoughtSpot, a platform design function built with engineering, not beside it: 140+ components and 12K weekly Figma activities, sustained by 8+ feature teams because the system made their work easier, not because anyone was told to use it.

03

Connection by design

At Yahoo!, a cross-vertical team set one navigation rule to unify a decade of disconnected surfaces, and I led the design of the surface that rule depended on most. Search touches every vertical, so making it move by the shared rule was the connection that held the platform together. The hardest part to fit is the part that proves the whole thing works.
