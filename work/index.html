Workiva
Senior UX Manager
2022 — Present

Shaping the decisions an enterprise makes before anything gets built.

I lead product UX across Financial Reporting, GRC, and Sustainability for over 75% of the Fortune 500: connected data, the document platform, and AI-driven automation for regulatory reporting. My design team is embedded across 10+ agile teams and 150+ engineers. The problem underneath was a distributed function where complexity outpaced any shared way to scope it, and decisions were reached before design entered the room. The work was operational coherence: the infrastructure that lets a function deliver without every decision running through one person. I authored the UX Project Sizing Framework, now used to scope complexity rather than guess at deadlines, and the Customer Value Roadmap, which made the design function's contribution legible to the business.

Apple / Claris
Design Manager
2012 — 2019

Owning where a platform's experience was going, and getting it there.

A packaged development platform more than 30 years old, with product lines that had grown apart into separate experiences sharing no common direction. Over eight years, I helped carry it into a cloud-native SaaS ecosystem: six products from zero to one, a cross-platform design system, and global reach across 16 countries and 12+ languages. Behind it, a design function built to carry a multi-year transformation without losing its direction.

ThoughtSpot
Director of Platform Design
2020 — 2022

Building the system an organization depends on.

A product growing faster than its design could stay consistent: feature teams working in isolation, duplicating patterns, with no one accountable for the system underneath. I led a platform design function built from nothing, with engineering rather than beside it: team, toolchain, component library, and the governance to keep it coherent. 140+ components and 12K weekly Figma activities, sustained by 8+ feature teams because the system made their work easier. The system outlasted my tenure.

The Spine
What ties the chapters together

Across these chapters, what I was accountable for kept widening: a platform's direction, then a system a whole organization depended on, then the decisions an enterprise makes before anything is built. The work stayed the same. The reach kept growing.

Global Experience
Design that works in every market

Global was a condition of the work from the first day. A design that only works in one market is not finished.

Apple / Claris

One platform, 16 countries

At Claris, I led design across 16 countries and 12+ languages, treating language, input method, and regional workflow as constraints from the first sketch, so decisions held in every market.

Yahoo!

One navigation, global markets

At Yahoo!, I led a global navigation revamp across desktop and mobile, tuned to each region's languages and habits, so one model could serve every market without flattening local differences.

Languages

Three languages, many regions

I speak English, Japanese, and Mandarin, and work with teams across continents and regions. Moving between them is how the work across markets actually got done.
