Workiva
Senior UX Manager
2022 — Present

Shaping the decisions an enterprise makes.

Leading product UX across Financial Reporting, GRC, and Sustainability for over 75% of the Fortune 500, spanning connected data, document platform, and AI driven automation for regulatory reporting, with a design team across multiple squads and 150+ engineers in all. The problem underneath was a distributed function where complexity outpaced any shared way to scope it, and decisions were reached before design entered the room. The work was operational coherence: the infrastructure that lets a function deliver without every decision running through one person. Authored the UX Project Sizing Framework, now used to scope complexity rather than guess at deadlines, and the Customer Value Roadmap, which made the design function's contribution legible to the business.

Apple / Claris
Design Manager
2012 — 2019

Owning a platform's direction and delivery.

A packaged development platform more than 30 years old, with product lines that had grown apart into separate experiences sharing no common direction. Carried into a cloud-native SaaS ecosystem over eight years: six products from zero to one, a cross-platform design system, and global reach across 16 countries and 12+ languages. The deepest proof of sustained leadership: a design function built to hold a multi-year transformation together without losing coherence.

ThoughtSpot
Director of Platform Design
2020 — 2022

Building the system an organization depends on.

A product growing faster than its design could stay consistent: feature teams working in isolation, duplicating patterns, with no one accountable for the system underneath. I led a platform design function built from nothing, with engineering rather than beside it: team, toolchain, component library, and the governance to keep it coherent. 140+ components and 12K weekly Figma activities, sustained by 8+ feature teams because the system made their work easier. The system outlasted the tenure.

Yahoo!
Design Lead, Search
2011 — 2012

Connecting a platform through its hardest surface.

A decade of disconnected surfaces unified into one platform: the cross-vertical team gave navigation, content, and search a single language across business units that each answered to their own numbers, and I led the Search design that made it the connection tying the verticals together. A separate global navigation revamp later drove $40M in quarterly revenue growth, traced directly to design decisions, not a cosmetic redesign.

Globalization at scale

At Claris, I led design across 16 countries and 12+ languages, treating language, input method, and regional workflow as design constraints from the start. Decisions had to hold across markets that did not think the same way.

Regional optimization
